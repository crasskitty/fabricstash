## Who is responsible

Bolt & Bobbin is published by Ashley Neale. She is the person to contact about this policy, at the email above, once she has filled it in.

## What the app does

Bolt & Bobbin is a catalog of sewing fabric. You keep photos, fiber, stretch, yardage in yards or meters, locations, and project notes for cloth you own. You can ask whether a piece is long enough for a project, deduct the length a project uses, and export a CSV.

## Where the catalog lives

Version 1 stores the catalog on the iPhone or iPad where you entered it. Photos you take or choose stay on that device as part of the catalog. Bolt & Bobbin does not upload the catalog, the photos, or a copy of the CSV to Ashley.

There is no account. Version 1 does not sync the catalog through iCloud. Removing the app removes the catalog from that device. A device backup you make yourself, and the CSV you export, are the copies you control.

## What Ashley does not do

Ashley does not collect the catalog. She does not collect usage analytics. She does not track you across apps or websites. She does not sell data. She does not buy data about you. The app has no advertising SDK and no account system.

## Purchases

The catalog is free for 25 fabrics. One purchase unlocks the rest. That purchase is a StoreKit 2 non-consumable. Apple processes the payment. Ashley does not receive your card number, and she does not run her own list of buyers. Apple’s privacy policy covers the purchase itself: [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Restore Purchase asks Apple, with your Apple Account, whether this unlock was already bought.

## CSV export

When you export a CSV, the share sheet is the system share sheet. Whatever you send, and whoever you send it to, is your action. Bolt & Bobbin does not take a copy of that export.

## Children

Bolt & Bobbin is a sewing catalog for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not ask anyone for an account or a profile.
