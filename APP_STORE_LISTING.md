## Description

```
Bolt & Bobbin is a catalog of the cloth you already own. Photograph a piece, write down what it is, and look it up before you buy another length you do not need.

Give each fabric photos, the fiber, and how it stretches. A short stretch-test guide is included so a knit gets measured the same way the next time. Record the yardage in yards or in meters, and the shelf, bin, or drawer where that piece lives.

Search and filter when you are in the shop or at the cutting table. Ask “Do I have enough?” before you cut. Name the project, enter the length it needs, and Bolt & Bobbin shows whether that piece covers it. When you sew the project, deduct the length you used. The yardage left on the piece stays honest.

Export the catalog as a CSV when you want a spreadsheet of your own.

Bolt & Bobbin opens onto your catalog. You do not create an account. Photos and notes stay on the iPhone or iPad where you entered them. The app does not track you. Version 1 keeps the catalog on that device.

The catalog is free for 25 fabrics. One purchase unlocks the rest. It is a one-time unlock. Restore that purchase with the same Apple Account if you move to a new phone.
```
