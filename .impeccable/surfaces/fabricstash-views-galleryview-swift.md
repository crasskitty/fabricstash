# Shelf

## Mode

Operate

## Audience and job

A sewist at the shelf, filing a piece of cloth or finding one. The job is to see the cloth, its color, and what is left, then add, edit, or cut.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shelf is a quiet grid of the cloth itself. The old brown studio, with its serif titles and madder buttons, is refused. The interface does not dye the fabric.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground (#E6EEF0, dark #152024), soft blue-charcoal ink, cards lifted one step off the paper. One sea-glass accent (#215E70, dark tint #9ED0DC) for actions and selection only. San Francisco for every label. Spacing on a 12-point measure. The cloth photo is the color field of each card.

STORY: The owner sees the shelf, opens a fabric, or adds one by taking a photo. They tap the cloth. A ring marks the spot, a swatch shows the sampled color, and that photo stays on the fabric. They never type a hex code.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navigation title FabricStash, search, and the add button. Under it, a short count line and a two-to-four column grid of cards. Each card is the photo, then the name, then the amount with a small swatch of the sampled color. Selection is an accent stroke. The primary action on an empty shelf is Add a fabric.

FORM: Coastal-morning inventory, fifth in the grounded list. The sample is a precise pixel reading (Ikeda), the photo is the color field and the accent never tints it (Metro), cards share one measure (Crouwel), the name and the amount sit in the same place on every card and the primary action is the accent button (airport signage), and the sampled spot is ringed (star atlas). seed:775c4e4dc4593c36

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Camera first, photo library second. One photo is the fabric photo and the color source. Stored color stays the existing hex value. Free shelf 25. Product id unchanged. No account, iCloud, CloudKit, analytics, or tracking. Yards, inches, GSM.
